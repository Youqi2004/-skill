# Platform Templates

## Xiaohongshu

Goal: seeding, search, saves, and trust.

Titles should feel like a real experience, discovery, or useful note. Use concrete user scenes and searchable keywords such as 智能戒指, 睡眠监测, 身体状态, HRV, 恢复, 熬夜, 轻薄佩戴.

Good title shapes:

- 戴智能戒指睡了 7 天，我终于知道为什么醒来还累
- 不想戴手表睡觉的人，可以看看 VOXU 智能戒指
- 智能戒指不是噱头，它适合记录这些身体趋势
- 熬夜后身体有没有恢复，看趋势比看感觉更可靠
- VOXU 智能戒指：把身体状态放进每天的复盘里

Body copy structure, unless the source has a stronger distinctive format:

1. Open with a relatable scene or problem.
2. Explain why the issue is hard to notice by feeling alone.
3. Introduce VOXU naturally as a lightweight trend-recording tool.
4. Mention 2-4 relevant functions in scenario language.
5. Close with soft interaction, such as inviting users to compare sleep, recovery, or wearing experience.

Do not make Xiaohongshu copy sound like a formal press release. Keep the source's paragraph rhythm when it feels native to Xiaohongshu. For dense review-style notes, a compact paragraph with "其实/但/一是/二是/如果" transitions is often better than a separated product explainer.

## Douyin

Goal: hook, completion, comments, and quick product recognition.

Titles should be more direct and curiosity-driven. Each title should focus on one problem, contradiction, or comparison.

Good title shapes:

- 智能戒指真的比手表更适合睡觉戴吗？
- 睡醒还是累？你可能只缺一个身体趋势记录
- 为什么越来越多人把健康监测戴在手上？
- 戴手表睡觉不舒服的人，可能会喜欢这种戒指
- 这枚 VOXU 智能戒指，记录的是你每天的身体状态

Post copy structure, unless the source has a stronger distinctive format:

1. First line: one sharp question or contradiction.
2. Middle: 2-3 short lines explaining the scene and VOXU value.
3. Product mention: name VOXU once or twice, not every line.
4. Ending: ask a comment-friendly question.

Keep Douyin copy compact. Use rhythm, not hype. If the source is a personal review or comparison, retain the same contradiction and reveal order instead of turning it into a generic hook list.

## Default Stage 1 Rules

- Always produce both Xiaohongshu and Douyin outputs unless the user asks for only one platform.
- Generate 5 title candidates per platform.
- Include one recommended title per platform.
- Include 2-3 cover headline options that are shorter than the post titles and suitable for large typography.
- Keep the source copy's useful structure or emotion, but write original VOXU copy.
