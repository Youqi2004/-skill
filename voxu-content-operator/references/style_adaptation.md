# Style Adaptation

## Core Rule

The user wants the source copy's expression and layout to be visibly echoed. Do not only extract the topic and then write a clean marketing template.

Before writing, silently identify the source's content DNA:

- Title device: clickbait, review diary, comparison, hot take, listicle, confession, or question.
- Narrator: first-person, brand voice, third-person observation, or creator review.
- Rhythm: long paragraph, short punch lines, "one is/two is" structure, parenthetical aside, or hashtag-heavy note.
- Transitions: words such as 其实, 但对我来说, 一是, 二是, 因为, 如果第一次, 最后留下, 不赘述.
- Detail density: price, place, usage duration, discomfort, loss, skin sensitivity, charging, water, sleep.
- Ending: recommendation, eligibility, soft caveat, comment question, or hashtag cluster.

Mimic those features in the VOXU version unless they conflict with truthfulness or brand safety.

## How To Rewrite Without Copying

- Keep the skeleton, replace the facts. If the source is "I bought several rings and kept one because comfort mattered most," VOXU can become "看了很多智能戒指后，真正影响长期佩戴的其实是舒适度."
- Keep characteristic phrases sparingly when they are generic platform language, such as "标题党一下", "但对我来说", "一是/二是". Do not copy distinctive personal facts or exact sentences.
- Preserve paragraph length. If the source is one dense Xiaohongshu paragraph with tabs or short breaks, write a compact note, not a polished article with many sections.
- Preserve uncertainty and caveats. If the source says "对我来说", keep a similar subjective restraint instead of absolute claims.
- Preserve concrete pain points. Convert "洗手后内里积水会过敏" into VOXU-safe product relevance such as "洗手、出汗后不想反复摘戴，所以日常防水和贴手感很重要."

## Avoid Mechanical Output

Do not default to formulaic official-account sentences such as:

- 很多人第一次看智能戒指，都会先问
- 它不是...而是...
- 适合这样的人
- 长期记录，让你更了解自己的变化
- 作为日常身体状态参考

These phrases are allowed only when the source format genuinely supports them. Prefer the source's own movement and sentence cadence.

Avoid over-explaining every function. If the source says "功能不赘述，最大的提升是佩戴体验," follow that move: briefly acknowledge functions, then spend most of the copy on wearing comfort.

## Source-Led Output Check

Before finalizing stage 1, check:

- Would the user recognize the source copy's format and tone in the rewrite?
- Did the VOXU content keep the same main emphasis instead of turning into a broad product intro?
- Are product claims softened enough to avoid medical or guaranteed-result wording?
- Is the copy natural enough for Xiaohongshu/Douyin, without sounding like a brochure?

If the answer to the first two is no, rewrite again with less templating and stronger source mimicry.
