# VOXU Brand And Claims

## Positioning

VOXU is an AI Smart Ring for daily body-state awareness. Content should make the product feel light, wearable, premium, and useful in ordinary life, especially for sleep, recovery, stress, activity, and habit review.

The core message is not "a medical device fixes your body." The core message is "a lightweight ring helps you observe body trends and understand yourself over time."

## Repeatable Selling Points

Use these points when they fit the source copy and scenario:

- Heart rate, blood oxygen, and HRV as daily body-state references.
- Skin temperature trends for observing subtle changes.
- Sleep and activity data for reviewing routines and lifestyle habits.
- Lightweight ring body, especially suitable for night wearing.
- Daily water resistance for washing hands, sweat, and ordinary daily use.
- Magnetic charging and about 4-6 days of battery life.
- Ring form factor: less screen distraction, easier to wear during sleep, more natural for long-term trend tracking.

## Tone

Default tone: steady seeding.

- Write like a real user-facing official account, not like a parameter sheet.
- When source copy has a distinctive voice, adapt VOXU into that voice instead of flattening it into a generic brand explanation.
- Put functions inside real scenarios: waking up tired, sleeping with a bulky watch, tracking recovery after exercise, noticing body-state trends after late nights.
- Keep the brand premium and restrained, but let the first sentence have a clear hook.
- Prefer short paragraphs, strong line breaks, and concrete scenes.

## Claim Boundaries

Avoid medicalized or exaggerated claims. Do not claim that VOXU can diagnose, treat, prevent, cure, or guarantee improvement.

Avoid wording like:

- 治疗失眠
- 诊断疾病
- 预防心梗
- 100% 准确
- 戴上就改善睡眠
- 医疗级监测
- 替代医生

Safer alternatives:

- 帮你观察睡眠趋势
- 作为日常身体状态参考
- 帮你复盘作息和恢复状态
- 让你更早注意到身体状态变化
- 长期记录，看到自己的变化趋势

When source copy makes a strong health promise, soften it into trend observation, habit review, or body-state awareness.
